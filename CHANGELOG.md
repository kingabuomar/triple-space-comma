# Changelog

All notable changes to Triple Space Comma are documented here.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-29

- Initial public release.
- Converts three quick spacebar taps into a comma and trailing space system-wide.
- Supports macOS double-space period both enabled and disabled.
- Includes a universal macOS app, login installer, recoverable uninstaller, unit tests, privacy documentation, and CI.
- Adds original project branding and app icon.
